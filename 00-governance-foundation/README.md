# Governance Foundation

**PROPOSED — pending formal review.** This directory is intended for the
governance model, adoption approach, accountability, and decision rights.
Its intended owner is a future governance sponsor or delegated governance
function; no owner or authority has been assigned.

The [governance architecture](../GOVERNANCE.md) connects the five layers and
defines artifact types. The [register schemas](../09-registers/README.md)
provide places to record authority, ownership, and applicability once decided.
